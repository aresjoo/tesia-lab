TETH AI Customer Relationship Summary

Effective September 28, 2026

Introduction

TETH AI ("TETH", "we", "our" or "us") provides an AI trading service in which an AI agent makes trading decisions and executes them according to a strategy the customer describes in plain language. TETH is not a broker-dealer or an exchange and does not hold customer assets. Every order is executed in the exchange account the customer connects (such as Bitget), and the assets stay in that exchange account.

Agentic trading works differently from traditional brokerage or investment advisory services. This summary explains what TETH does and does not do, how you pay, and what conflicts of interest exist.

What services can you provide me?

  • Strategy building. TETH turns the investing idea you describe in chat into a strategy with explicit entry, exit and stop-loss rules, and shows how it would have performed on historical market data.
  • Agentic trading. For each strategy you activate, an AI agent reads market conditions, decides when to buy and sell, and places the orders. The AI agent can execute trades without the customer intervening in each individual trade. Tradable instruments are the stocks, options and crypto assets supported by the connected exchange.
  • Strategy following. You can review the decision log of a published strategy and run the same strategy in your own account within a budget you set.
  • Scope of permissions. TETH uses read and order permissions only and never requests withdrawal permission. You set the trading budget and limits, and you can pause a strategy or disconnect the exchange at any time. Every decision the AI agent makes is recorded together with its reasoning.

TETH has no account minimum. Whether a strategy is activated, and how much budget it receives, is always your decision.

What fees will I pay?

  • TETH Direct. A subscription of KRW 599,000 per month gives you every feature with the exchange account you already use.
  • TETH Partner. Using a partner exchange account, the subscription fee is KRW 0. Partner includes the same features as Direct, and you can switch to Direct at any time.
  • Exchange costs. Trading fees, spreads, funding rates, margin interest and similar costs are charged by the connected exchange under its own schedule. TETH does not charge a per-trade fee.
  • AI usage. If you exceed the AI usage included in your plan, additional charges may apply at the rates shown on the usage screen in the app.

You will pay fees and costs whether you make or lose money, and over time they reduce the amount you earn. Current pricing is shown in the app and on the website, and changes are reflected there.

What are your obligations to me, and what conflicts of interest do you have?

TETH places orders only within the strategy, budget and limits you set, and never holds or moves your assets. At the same time, the way we make money can create conflicts with your interests. Examples include:

  • Partner exchange arrangement. The TETH Partner plan waives the subscription fee through an arrangement with partner exchanges. This gives us an incentive to encourage you to use a partner exchange.
  • Subscription revenue. The more Direct subscribers we have, the more we earn, so we have an incentive to encourage you to keep subscribing.
  • AI model costs. We combine several AI models depending on the situation, and each model costs a different amount. We have an incentive to choose cheaper models. To mitigate this, models are selected on decision quality and the model used is recorded in the decision log.

What are the limitations of the AI agent?

AI agents can make mistakes, misinterpret instructions, act on incomplete or outdated information, or behave in unexpected ways. AI-driven strategies can underperform in particular market conditions, and in volatile markets it may be difficult to intervene or stop them in real time.

We do not guarantee the accuracy, completeness or suitability of the AI agent's decisions, and we are not responsible for losses that result from decisions the agent generates. You are responsible for reviewing account activity, monitoring positions, and confirming that the agent is behaving as intended.

How do your employees make money?

TETH employees receive salaries and bonuses based on their responsibilities and performance. No compensation is tied to the number or size of customer trades or to trading in any particular asset.

Do you have legal or disciplinary history?

No.
