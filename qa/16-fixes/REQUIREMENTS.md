## Shared state model (built once, used by 01, 08, 09, 10, 13, 14)
- `INTENT`: `{kind: 'bt'|'copy'|'activate'|'terminal', id, ex}` stored in session, consumed once after auth or activation.
- `ACCESS`: derived `none | paid | partner` from `payDone`, `uidLinked`.
- `EXCHANGE`: `{selected, connected, pending, error}` with `connected` a list, so "different exchange connected" is a state, not a guess.
- `BILLING`: `none | paid | failed | cancelled | partner`.
- Config in one place: supported exchanges, partner benefit rate per exchange, price 280 USD.
